# Coat Customizer

Give your customers the freedom to create a coat that feels truly their own.

Coat Customizer is a clean, interactive shopping experience that lets customers explore different coats and button styles in real time. They can change the coat without losing their selected buttons, or try another button style while keeping the same coat. Every choice appears instantly on the product preview, making customization simple, visual, and enjoyable.

## Why it stands out

- **Interactive shopping experience** - Customers see their choices immediately.
- **Independent customization** - Coat and button styles can be changed separately.
- **Easy product expansion** - New coats and buttons are added automatically from organized image folders.
- **Built for growing collections** - Scrolling and pagination keep large catalogs easy to browse.
- **Responsive design** - The experience works beautifully across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- **Smooth and polished** - Loading states and clear selection indicators keep every interaction professional.

## Perfect for

- Bespoke tailoring businesses
- Fashion retailers
- Made-to-order clothing brands
- Online suit and coat stores
- Product customization demonstrations

Coat Customizer transforms a standard product page into a personal styling experience. It helps customers explore more combinations, feel confident in their choices, and connect with the product before placing an order.
